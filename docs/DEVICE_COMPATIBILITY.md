## 4. Decisions that must wait for this inventory

- Ktor vs NanoHTTPD (minimum API).
- Whether Plan A (LiteRT.js WebGPU), Plan B (native GPU) or Plan C (other model) is possible.
- Capture resolution (720p vs 480p) and stream resolution/fps.
- Hotspot host and exact IP-discovery logic.
- Inference rate cap and thermal degradation thresholds.
- Whether gyro/accelerometer logging is real or empty, and at what rate.
- Whether `devicemotion` needs a native sensor path.

## 5. Rules

Record the date, app commit hash and procedure beside every measurement. Mark confidence (measured / inferred / reported). Do not generalise one device's result to others.

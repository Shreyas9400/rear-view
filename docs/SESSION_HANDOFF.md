## 6. Decisions and unresolved questions

- D-001 to D-017 in `TECHNICAL_DECISIONS.md`. New this session: D-014 (layout, accepted), D-015 (toolchain observed), D-016 (decode class rule, proposed), D-017 (npm audit, accepted risk). None of the design decisions are `verified`.

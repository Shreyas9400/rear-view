# v0.5.1 (experimental pre-release)

> **Experimental research prototype, not a safety system.** Not certified, not validated, it misses vehicles and people and gives false alarms. Do not rely on it for driving decisions. No warning does not mean the road is clear.

A signed, non-debug Android APK for the **rear phone**. The viewer is a web page the phone serves; open it in Safari on an iPhone (or any browser) on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.

## Download and check

- File: `rearview-v0.5.1.apk` (about 25 MB), version 0.5.1 (code 501), Android 7.0 or newer.
- APK SHA-256: `21b6ce888683689e6f10287dd1a426747823de23c5f65d91a0fcf030c6884419`

Verify on a computer: `sha256sum rearview-v0.5.1.apk` (or `certutil -hashfile rearview-v0.5.1.apk SHA256` on Windows).

## Install in five steps

1. Copy the APK to the rear phone and open it (allow "install unknown apps" when asked). Uninstall any debug build you made yourself first.
2. Open **Rear-View Assistant** and allow the camera. It starts by itself.
3. Switch on the rear phone's hotspot and join your iPhone to it (or use any shared Wi-Fi for a first test).
4. Scan the QR code on the rear screen with the iPhone camera and open the link in Safari. Bookmark the address.
5. Tap "Enable sound" in the viewer. Read [docs/INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Shreyas9400/rear-view/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) for calibration, cooling and troubleshooting.

## What is in it

Camera, on-device YOLOX-nano detection of vehicles and people, tracking, alerts (possible approach, vehicle very close, person close, detection unavailable), left/right direction with a mirror option, proximity beeps, red outline, full-screen mode, a local server, QR pairing with a remembered key, an approximate distance with calibration.

## Known limits

- Tested on one OnePlus 6T and one iPhone. Not field-tested in traffic; no scored accuracy; only a few minutes of testing on a real clip.
- It only knows people, cars, motorcycles, buses and trucks. Walls, poles, kerbs and animals raise nothing.
- Distance is a rough estimate from box size. Alert points need calibrating for your mount.
- Phones overheat in a rear window; a cooler made a large difference in our room tests.
- Plain HTTP on a local network: anyone on that network with the address and key can watch.
- Hotspot name `rearview.local` is untested; use the address and QR code.
